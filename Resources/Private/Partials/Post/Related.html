Renders the related posts of a post as teasers: the hand-picked related posts of the page record if there are any, otherwise the posts sharing the most tags (settings.related.limit) below settings.related.pages (comma separated root page uids, default: the parent page of the post). Variables: post (Page), settings (plugin settings). Section "Related": posts is either the object storage of hand-picked pages or the result of the ViewHelper, an array of {post, amount}.
